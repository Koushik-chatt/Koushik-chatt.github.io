Educating the next generation

Lectures

Teaching brings the ideas of gravity, accretion, and computational astrophysics into focus through physical intuition and examples. Lectures and public discussions connect fundamental concepts with the simulations and observations used to explore the universe.

Mentorship

Mentorship creates space to ask questions, develop independence, and work through the challenges of research. Through discussion and collaborative projects, developing researchers build experience with numerical simulations and interpreting the physics they reveal.
